Alphabet · 3 · Danger zone

Danger zone: letters we often mix up

A · E · I top mistake

A = /eɪ/ · E = /iː/ · I = /aɪ/

G vs J

G = /dʒiː/ like see · J = /dʒeɪ/ like say

H

H = /eɪtʃ/

R

R = /ɑː/ like car

W & Y

W = double-u · Y = /waɪ/ like why

B · P · V

B /biː/ · P /piː/ · V /viː/

F L M N S X ending

ef · el · em · en · es · eks

Q vs U · Z

Q = /kjuː/ · U = /juː/ · Z = zed (UK) / zee (US)
