⭐ Grammar 1 · Main · Theory

Past simple questions · did · was · were

📘 Câu hỏi ở quá khứ đơn với did / was / were — để hỏi thêm chi tiết câu chuyện
subjectbe / auxverbkey word
❓ Did…?
Did+subject+base verb+…?
Yes, I did./No, I didn't.
Câu hỏi Có / Không với động từ thường
Did you miss the bus? — Yes, I did.
Did she find her phone? — No, she didn't.
❓ Wh- + did
Where / What / When / Who+did+subject+base verb?
Hỏi thông tin: ở đâu, cái gì, khi nào, với ai
Where did you go?
What did you do next?
❓ Was / Were…?
Was / Were+subject+adjective / place?
Yes, I was./No, we weren't.
Câu hỏi với be (tính từ, nơi chốn)
Were you late? — Yes, I was.
Was the bus full?
❓ What happened?
What / Who+past verb+…?
Hỏi về chủ ngữ — KHÔNG dùng did
What happened next?
Who helped you?
💡 RememberSau did → động từ nguyên mẫu: ✅ Did you go? ❌ Did you went?Không dùng did với be: ❌ Did you late? ✅ Were you late?❌ What did happen? ✅ What happened?

Grammar 2 · Also in this lesson · Theory

Past continuous · while I was…

📘 Quá khứ tiếp diễn: hành động đang diễn ra thì một việc khác xảy ra
subjectbe / auxverbkey word
⏳ was / were + -ing
I / He / She / It+was+verb-ing
You / We / They+were+verb-ing
Đang làm gì (tại một thời điểm trong quá khứ)
I was waiting for the bus.
We were travelling in Hue.
⚡ while + long action, short action
While+subject+was / were+verb-ing,past simple
Trong khi đang … (dài) thì … (ngắn) xảy ra
While I was waiting for the bus, it started to rain.
While we were travelling, I lost my phone.
🕰 Long vs short
⏳ was walking+⚡ met
Hành động dài ⏳ (quá khứ tiếp diễn) · hành động ngắn ⚡ (quá khứ đơn)
While I was walking home, I met an old friend.
💡 Remembertravel → travelling · wait → waiting · have → havingDấu phẩy sau mệnh đề while khi nó đứng đầu câu

Grammar 3 · Also in this lesson · Theory

Story linkers · at first → in the end

📘 Từ nối kể chuyện theo trình tự: mở đầu – vấn đề – may mắn – kết thúc
subjectbe / auxverbkey word
🔢 Story order
At first,+Suddenly,+Fortunately,
Eventually,+In the end,
Lúc đầu → đột nhiên → may mắn thay → cuối cùng
At first, everything was fine. Suddenly, I realised the mistake.
Fortunately, I found my wallet. In the end, I got home.
🔍 Meaning
eventually=after a long time
in the end=the last part of the story
suddenly = bất ngờ · fortunately = may mắn · eventually = sau một thời gian dài
We waited for an hour. Eventually, the bus arrived.
In the end, we got home at midnight.
💡 RememberLinker đứng đầu câu + dấu phẩy + ngắt hơi ‖
