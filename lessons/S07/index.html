Grammar · Theory

would like · will · could

📘 Ba động từ khuyết thiếu dùng khi lịch sự: would like (muốn) · will (quyết định ngay) · could (yêu cầu)
subjectbe / auxverbkey word
🍽 would like
I'd like+a / the + noun
I'd like+to+verb
I'd like + danh từ · I'd like to + động từ (I'd = I would)
I'd like a glass of water.
I'd like to order now, please.
⚡ will ('ll)
I'll+verb+…
Quyết định ngay lúc nói · will + động từ nguyên mẫu
It's hot today. I'll have an iced tea.
I'll pay by card.
🙏 could
Could+I / we / you+verb+…, please?
Yêu cầu lịch sự · Could + chủ ngữ + động từ nguyên mẫu
Could you bring some napkins, please?
Could we sit by the window?
🤵 Would you like…?
Would you like+noun / to + verb?
Yes, please./No, thank you.
Lời mời / đề nghị · trả lời lịch sự
Would you like to see the menu? — Yes, please.
Would you like some more tea? — No, thank you.
💡 Remember❌ I want a coffee. (hơi cộc) → ✅ I'd like a coffee, please.Sau will / could / would → động từ nguyên mẫu: ❌ Could you brings…

Input · Polite ordering

Make it polite

I'd like…polite wish
I'd like the tomato soup, please.
I'll have…decide now
I'll have the fish and chips.
Could I…? / Could we…?polite request
Could I have it without onions?
💡 Say please and go up ↗ at the end of a request.
😐 Give me the menu.
😊 Could I see the menu, please?
😐 I want water.
😊 I'd like some water, please.
😐 Chicken.
😊 I'll have the chicken, please.
😐 No onions!
😊 Could I have it without onions, please?
😐 What is good?
😊 What do you recommend?
😐 Bill!
😊 Could we have the bill, please?

Structure 1 / 4

What do you recommend?

🇻🇳 Bạn gợi ý món gì?
→ I recommend the ___. It's very ___.
🇻🇳 Tôi gợi ý món ___. Món này rất ___.
QWhat do you recommend?
💬 Common answers
a
I recommend the grilled chicken. It's very popular.Tôi gợi ý món gà nướng. Món này rất được ưa chuộng.
b
The fish and chips is very good today.Món cá và khoai tây chiên hôm nay rất ngon.
c
For a starter, I recommend the tomato soup.Về món khai vị, tôi gợi ý súp cà chua.
⚡ Translate now · Dịch ngay
1🇻🇳 Bạn gợi ý món tráng miệng nào?
🇬🇧 What dessert do you recommend?
2🇻🇳 Bạn có thể gợi ý một món cho tôi không?
🇬🇧 Can you recommend a dish for me?
3🇻🇳 Tôi gợi ý món cà ri rau.
🇬🇧 I recommend the vegetable curry.

Structure 2 / 4

I'll have ___. / I'd like ___.

🇻🇳 Tôi sẽ gọi ___. / Tôi muốn ___.
→ To start with, I'll have ___. For the main course, I'd like ___.
🇻🇳 Trước tiên, tôi gọi ___. Món chính, tôi muốn ___.
QAre you ready to order?
💬 Common answers
a

b

c

🇬🇧 For the main course, I'd like the vegetable curry.

Structure 3 / 4

Could I have it without ___?

🇻🇳 Tôi có thể dùng món đó mà không có ___ không?
→ Of course. / I'm sorry, it comes with ___.
🇻🇳 Tất nhiên. / Xin lỗi, món này có kèm ___.
QCould I have the burger without onions?
💬 Common answers
a
Of course. No problem.Tất nhiên. Không vấn đề gì.
b
Sure. Do you have a food allergy?Được. Anh/chị có dị ứng thực phẩm không?
c
I'm sorry, the curry comes with nuts.Xin lỗi, món cà ri có kèm các loại hạt.
⚡ Translate now · Dịch ngay
1🇻🇳 Tôi có thể dùng món salad mà không có phô mai không?
🇬🇧 Could I have the salad without cheese?
2🇻🇳 Tôi bị dị ứng thực phẩm. Tôi không ăn được các loại hạt.
🇬🇧 I have a food allergy. I can't eat nuts.
3🇻🇳 Cho tôi món cá mà không có khoai tây chiên được không?
🇬🇧 Could I have the fish without chips?

Pronunciation

could‿I · I’ll‿have — polite tone ↗

🔗 Link the words & follow the arrow
🔗 Linking

could‿I = /kʊˈdaɪ/

I'll‿have = /aɪlˈhæv/

would‿you = /wʊˈdʒuː/

🎵 Polite tone

↗ requests & questions: Could I…? Would you…?

↘ orders: I'll have the fish, please.

Flat voice = rude. Friendly voice = polite! 🙂
